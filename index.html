How I work

Short process, explicit decisions, clean handoff.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    I learn the context, constraints, and urgency. If it’s not a fit, I’ll say so immediately.

  2. 02

    Scope and proposal

    Goal, deliverables, timeline, and engagement model (sprint or fractional). No ambiguity.

  3. 03

    Build and checkpoints

    I work in milestones. You see progress, risks, and technical choices as they happen.

  4. 04

    Ship and handoff

    Release, essential documentation, and transfer to your team. Ready for the next step.
